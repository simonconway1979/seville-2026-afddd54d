Food

The Seville specialities worth trying, and where we might find them.

Seville eats standing up, one tapa at a time. Here’s what’s worth trying, and some places we might go.

Things to try

Fried fish
- Pescaíto frito: mixed fried fish in a paper cone. Easy for Freya.
- Cazón en adobo: fish marinated in cumin and vinegar, then fried. The most Sevillian of the lot.

Classic tapas
- Espinacas con garbanzos: spinach and chickpeas with cumin. Seville’s signature tapa.
- Salmorejo: a thick, cold tomato cream, richer than gazpacho.
- Montadito de pringá: a little roll of slow-cooked meats. Pure comfort food.
- Carrillada: pork cheeks, braised until they fall apart.
- Solomillo al whisky: pork in a garlic and whisky sauce. Invented here.
- Tortilla: potato omelette, for Freya.

Breakfast
- Tostada: toast with olive oil and grated tomato. How Seville starts the day.
- Churros with thick hot chocolate. (Freya)

Sweets
- Huesos de santo and buñuelos: marzipan “bones” and cream doughnuts, only around All Saints’.
- Convent sweets: nuns sell cakes through a turning hatch, so you never see them.
- Tortas de aceite: crisp olive oil biscuits, sold everywhere.

To drink
- Manzanilla or fino: very cold, with fried fish.
- Vermouth on tap before Sunday lunch.
- Cruzcampo, the local beer, or tinto de verano.

Places we might go

Eslava · Calle Eslava 3
The city’s favourite tapas bar: traditional cooking with a modern twist.

El Rinconcillo · Calle Gerona 40
The oldest bar in Seville, all tiles and dark wood, where they chalk the bill on the counter.

Bar Alfalfa · Calle Candilejo 1
Tiny, always packed, and one of the best-rated bars in the city.

Las Golondrinas · Calle Pagés del Corro 76
A family-run Triana classic since 1962, covered in the neighbourhood’s tiles.

Mercado de Triana · Calle San Jorge 6
A food market with bars inside, built on the ruins of a castle.

Casa Morales · Calle García de Vinuesa 11
Open since 1850, with huge clay jars along the walls, near the Cathedral. Closed Sundays.

Freiduría La Isla · Calle García de Vinuesa 13
Fried fish in a paper cone, right next door to Casa Morales.

Bodega Santa Cruz · Calle Rodrigo Caro 1
A marble standing bar at the edge of Santa Cruz, for a quick montadito.

Casa Vizcaíno · Calle Feria 27
An old neighbourhood bar near the hotel, known for its homemade vermouth.

Cañabota · Calle José Gestoso 19
The best seafood in Seville, grilled in front of you at the bar.

Bar El Comercio · Calle Lineros 9
Churros and hot chocolate since 1904. Closed Sundays.
