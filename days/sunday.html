Sunday

Sun 1 Nov · All Saints' Day
The Alcázar, Plaza de España, lunch in Triana, a quiet afternoon.

A slow day with one big sight in the morning, and a long break in the middle.

Ideas for Sunday

The Real Alcázar · morning
A royal palace of tiled halls and carved ceilings. The gardens have fountains, a maze and peacocks. Best first thing, before the crowds. (Freya)

Plaza de España
A huge half-circle of tiled bridges, and rowing boats on the canal. (Freya)

Lunch in Triana
Across the river to Las Golondrinas, a family-run bar covered in local tiles.

An afternoon at the hotel
A massage for you, while Freya and I swim on the roof.

Las Setas at sunset
A walk across the top of the giant wooden canopy, a few minutes from the hotel. Then a tapa nearby if we’re hungry.

Good to know

  • It’s All Saints’ Day, a national holiday. The Alcázar and the bars above are open.
  • Alcázar tickets are timed, so we’d book ahead
  • Look for huesos de santo and buñuelos in the bakery windows
