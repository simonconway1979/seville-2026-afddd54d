Sat 31 Oct · the birthday day
Tapas at Eslava, flamenco at five, drinks on the roof.

Freya and I land before 11:00. Nothing’s fixed. Here are the things I think you’d love.

Ideas for Saturday

Settle in at the hotel
Drop the bags, find you, and take a first look at the courtyards.

Lunch at Eslava · around 13:00
The tapas bar locals rate highest, 15 minutes’ walk away. The honey ribs and the artichokes are the famous ones.

Flamenco · 17:00
An hour-long show at the Flamenco Dance Museum: guitar, song, and dancing close enough to hear the heels. The early show is the family one, and Freya goes free. (Freya)

Drinks on the roof
Back to the hotel as the lights come on.

Good to know

  • Check-in is from 15:00, so the bags go to the desk first
  • For flamenco, arrive around 16:15 for good seats
  • The flamenco museum is 10–15 minutes’ walk from the hotel
